Visualization 2 — Movie Performance and Audience Discussion

Each bubble is a movie with box-office data (2,745 movies; streaming-first releases with no box office are left out). Bubble size is box office and color is primary genre. Rating vs. discussion puts audience rating against how much a movie is talked about, compared with movies released around the same time. The median lines split out hidden gems (high rating, little discussion), acclaimed hits, and movies talked about despite low ratings. Rating vs. review tone asks whether written reviews are as warm as the rating; movies far from the diagonal are where the two disagree.
