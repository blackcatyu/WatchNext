Actor & Director Collaboration Network

This network shows repeated collaboration relationships between actors and directors in our movie dataset. It focuses on stronger collaborations so that the overall structure remains readable in a static view.

The current static view includes people with at least five movies and actor–director pairs that worked together on at least three movies. Circles represent actors and diamonds represent directors. Larger nodes have stronger overall collaboration relationships, while thicker edges represent pairs that worked together more often. Colors distinguish collaboration communities; people in the same community share a color.
