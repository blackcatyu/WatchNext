Dataset

Raw datasets

  • MovieLens 32M — 32,000,204 ratings and 2,000,072 free-text tags from 200,948 users, across 87,585 movies (Jan 1995–Oct 2023). grouplens.org
  • TMDB API — movie metadata (genres, budget, revenue, ratings), cast/crew credits, keywords, and user reviews, pulled per-movie via the TMDB API. developer.themoviedb.org

Processed dataset

2,914 candidate movies, built in two tracks and merged:

  • Historical (1,998 movies, 1922–2019) — selected from MovieLens by rating count, so each one has real MovieLens user-rating data (needed for the audience-similarity network below).
  • Recent (916 movies, 2016–2026) — MovieLens's ratings stop in Oct 2023 and thin out well before that for newer releases, so this track is discovered directly from TMDB (by vote count) to cover the years MovieLens can't see.

Each movie carries TMDB metadata, top-10 billed cast, director(s), and TMDB keywords. 11,481 TMDB reviews (covering ~2,650 of the 2,914 movies) were cleaned, deduplicated, and scored for sentiment.

Data cleaning / processing completed

  • Parsed release year and genre list out of MovieLens's raw title/genre strings; normalized IMDb IDs (zero-padded, since leading zeros are significant).
  • Validated and deduplicated MovieLens ratings; converted timestamps to real dates.
  • Selected the two-track candidate set described above and pulled matching TMDB records for each movie.
  • Deduplicated movies that appeared in both tracks (kept the MovieLens-linked copy) and clipped a handful of implausible TMDB budget/revenue values (crowd-edited fields occasionally carry vandalized placeholder numbers).
  • Cleaned review text (stripped HTML/URLs, dropped near-empty/duplicate reviews), scored sentiment with siebert/sentiment-roberta-large-english (chosen over a tweet-tuned model since our reviews run ~170 words at the median, far past a tweet's length), and extracted per-movie keywords via TF-IDF.

Visualizations

Visualization 1 — Audience Similarity Network

Every movie in the dataset as a star. The 2,759 movies MovieLens users rated are placed by audience overlap (UMAP on 1 − Jaccard of their ≥4/5 fan bases), so movies liked by the same people sit close together. The 155 newest releases have no MovieLens data and appear as four-point stars placed beside the movies they most resemble in content. Brightness = TMDB vote count. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover for nearest neighbors, click a star to zoom in (click it again to zoom back out). Try Color by: Decade: shared audiences follow release era more than genre.

Visualization 2 — Movie Performance and Audience Discussion

Each bubble is a movie with box-office data (2,745 movies; streaming-first releases with no box office are left out). Size = box office, color = primary genre. Rating vs. discussion: audience rating against how much the movie is discussed (TMDB votes compared with movies released within ±2 years, so older movies' extra decades of votes don't dominate); median lines split out hidden gems, acclaimed hits and movies talked about despite low ratings. Rating vs. review tone: rating percentile against the share of positive written reviews (movies with ≥3 reviews); distance from the diagonal is disagreement. Pick a region in the side panel to list its most typical movies; click a bubble for director, cast and review keywords.
