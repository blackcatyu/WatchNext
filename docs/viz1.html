Visualization 1 — Audience Similarity Network

Two movies count as similar when many of the same MovieLens users rated both highly (≥4/5). Universe shows every movie at once as a star map, with movies that share fans close together. The audience network shows 80 movies at a time, where edge thickness is the share of the two movies' combined fans who liked both (Jaccard overlap). Both include 2016+ releases that MovieLens users rated before October 2023. Newer movies with no MovieLens data appear as four-point stars placed by content similarity and in the "newest releases" sidebar. Search any of ~2,900 movies above.
