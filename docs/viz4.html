Genre Ratings Over Time

Compare how 13 major genres are rated, how many TMDB votes they receive, and how many movies appear in the dataset from 2016 through the partial 2026 release year.

Vote-count-weighted average of TMDB movie ratings

Years: 2016–2026*
Genres: 13
Metric: Weighted Average Rating
Source: TMDB

Hover a cell to compare its genre row and release-year column

Each cell is one genre–year combination. The default color is the vote-count-weighted average of TMDB movie ratings, not a pooled set of raw user-level ratings. Movies with multiple genres contribute to every relevant row. 2026* contains partial-year data, so its counts should not be interpreted as a complete annual decline.
